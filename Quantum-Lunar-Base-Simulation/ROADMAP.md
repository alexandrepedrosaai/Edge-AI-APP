Quantum Lunar Base Simulation - ROADMAP

🌌 Overview

This roadmap outlines the progressive development of the Quantum Lunar Base Simulation project. Each phase introduces new capabilities, expanding from local sustainability to interstellar communication.

🚀 Phases

Phase 1: Sustainability → Oxygen, water, and dome constants.

Phase 2: Temporal Simulation → 30-day tracking of resources.

Phase 3: Probabilistic Modeling → Random variations and system failures.

Phase 4: Resource Optimization → Recycling and energy efficiency.

Phase 5: Expansion → Scaling dome and resources for 1000 humans.

Phase 6: Quantum Governance → Distributed decision-making protocols.

Phase 7: Orbital Integration → Communication with satellites.

Phase 8: Interplanetary Integration → Communication with Mars bases.

Phase 9: Interstellar Integration → Hypothetical quantum communication with colonies beyond the solar system.

Phase 10: Future Research → AI-driven optimization, hybrid quantum-classical simulations, and governance frameworks.

📊 Purpose

This roadmap provides a clear vision for the evolution of the project, ensuring that sustainability, scalability, and connectivity remain at the core of lunar and interplanetary colonization research.
